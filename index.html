Inside the pouch

What goes into a pouch?

A modern smoke-free pouch is built from a few simple parts: nicotine, water, flavouring, and plant fibres. It is made for adults who want a tobacco-free pouch used in a similar way to snus.

Shop products
  • Nicotine The active part
  • Water Keeps it soft
  • Flavouring Taste profile
  • Plant fibres Soft pouch body

How it works

Simple use guide

  1. 01 Use one pouch per session.
  2. 02 Place it under your upper lip.
  3. 03 Keep it in place for about 60 seconds, or up to 5 minutes if preferred.
  4. 04 Put the used pouch into the lid compartment before disposal.
